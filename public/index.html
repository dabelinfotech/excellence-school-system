Nursery & Primary Wing

Excellence Elementary School

🧸 Age: 18 Months – 11 Years (Creche, Nursery 1-2, Basic 1-6)

Nurturing inquisitive young minds through the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) and Key Stages 1 & 2 British National Curriculum. Emphasis on Jolly Phonics, Singapore Maths, STEM discovery, creativity, and moral discipline.

  • Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) & British Phonics Framework
  • Singapore Maths Mastery & Hands-on Junior STEM Laboratories
  • Dedicated Elementary Result Checker & Interactive KS2 Quizzes
  • Daily Parent Reading Diary, Meal Tracking & Pastoral Care

Secondary & Sixth Form

Excellence College

🔬 Age: 11 – 18 Years (JSS 1-3, SSS 1-3 & British A-Levels)

Cultivating academic excellence and disciplined leadership through Key Stage 3, Cambridge IGCSE, WAEC/NECO, and Cambridge Advanced Level (A-Level) pathways for world-leading university matriculation.

  • Dual British Curriculum (IGCSE/A-Levels) & Nigerian WAEC Certification
  • State-of-the-Art Sciences, ICT, Robotics & AI Complex
  • High-Stakes Computer-Based Test (CBT) Timed Exam Simulator
