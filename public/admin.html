👑 Super Admin (Main Admin)

Master Control

The supreme administrative authority (Director General & Head of Schools). Holds master root privileges:

  • Create, edit, and deactivate Staff & Moderator accounts
  • Permanently delete student records and wipe erroneous scores
  • Configure School Session, Current Term, and Tuition Fees
  • Approve Bursary receipts, generate PINs, and issue clearance certificates
  • Publish bulletin notices to the live homepage ticker tape

🛡️ Staff Moderator

Operational Clearance

Operational and wing supervisors (Academic Deans, Housemasters, Bursar Clerks):

  • Enrol new students and update student profiles
  • Audit bank receipts and approve student fee payments
  • Record subject assessment scores and view terminal results
  • Manage CBT question banks and supervise online quiz attempts
  • Protected: Cannot delete students, modify other staff accounts, or alter school core settings.
